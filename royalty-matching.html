🔎 Royalty Matching Engine

Recovering unmatched streaming revenue for a North African label

📋 About This Project

DSP statements rarely arrive clean. ISRCs come with dashes or typos, or are missing entirely, and artist names appear in Latin script, Darija transliteration or Arabic. Every line that cannot be matched to the catalogue is revenue that never reaches the rights holder.

This engine matches 317 statement lines from 5 DSPs across 7 territories to the catalogue of a fictional label, Atlas Sound Records, in three steps: ISRC matching, artist + title matching through an alias table, and duplicate detection. The full model is built in Excel with standard formulas (INDEX/MATCH, SUMIFS, COUNTIF); this page runs the same logic live in your browser.

⚠️ All data is fictional. Atlas Sound Records, its artists and its tracks do not exist; revenues are simulated.
